World Overview

Ashfall Peninsula is where the outbreak began. Airborne spores drift inland, mutate, weaken electronics, and cause hallucinations and unpredictable undead behavior. No place is fully safe.

Core Mechanic — Real-World Movement

Wherever you physically walk becomes the next in-game zone. The DM converts your real surroundings into the fiction — placing zombies, spores, hazards, loot, and faction presence based on what's actually around you.
